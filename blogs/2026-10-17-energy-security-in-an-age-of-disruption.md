While there is no single prescription for energy security, strengthening “response capacity,” such as by establishing stockpiles, substituting sources, and diversifying suppliers, is essential. The point is not to eliminate vulnerabilities altogether, but to ensure that there are options when disruption arrives, write Diego Hernandez Diaz, Luciano Di Fiori, and Tiago Devesa in Project Syndicate.
