Economic relations between Poland and the United States have entered a new phase. Over the past three decades, cooperation has expanded beyond trade to encompass investment, technology, infrastructure, energy, and defense. A new report by McKinsey and the American Chamber of Commerce in Poland, drawing on survey data, identifies six priority sectors and outlines actions that businesses and governments can take to sustain this momentum.
